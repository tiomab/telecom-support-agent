## No internet connection — troubleshooting
Before opening a ticket, the customer should try these steps in order:
1. Check for a known outage in the customer's region.
2. Check that the NovaBox router is powered on and the "Fibre" LED is green.
3. Restart the router: unplug it for 30 seconds, then plug it back in and wait 3 minutes.
4. If the "Fibre" LED is red or blinking, check that the fibre cable is firmly connected to the wall socket and not bent.
5. Connect a computer with an Ethernet cable to rule out a Wi-Fi problem.
If none of these steps help and there is no known outage, a technical ticket should be opened.

## Slow internet
Speeds over Wi-Fi are usually lower than over cable. For speed tests, use a wired connection.
Wi-Fi 5 GHz band is faster but has shorter range; 2.4 GHz has longer range but is slower.
If wired speed is below 50% of the plan speed for more than 24 hours, a technical ticket should be opened.

## Technician visits
A technician visit is free if the fault is on NovaTel's side. If the fault is caused by customer equipment or damage at the customer's premises, the visit costs 25 EUR.
