# Outages and Compensation

## Planned maintenance
Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance by SMS and in the app. It usually happens between 01:00 and 05:00.

## Unplanned outages
During a known outage, customers do not need to open a ticket: the issue is already being worked on, and they should wait for the estimated restoration time.
Customers can see active outages in the app (Help > Network status).

## Compensation
If a home internet or mobile service is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours because of a NovaTel fault, the customer is entitled to compensation of 1/30 of the monthly fee for each full day of outage.
Compensation is applied automatically on the next invoice for known outages. For faults not linked to a known outage, the customer must open a ticket within 30 days.
