# SIM and eSIM

## Lost or stolen SIM
The SIM should be blocked immediately to prevent misuse. Blocking can be done in the NovaTel app (Account > SIM > Block) or by support.
A replacement SIM costs 5 EUR and is sent within 2 working days, or can be picked up the same day in a NovaTel shop with an ID document.

## Switching to eSIM
eSIM is free of charge. The customer requests it in the app (Account > SIM > Switch to eSIM) and receives a QR code by email.
After the eSIM is activated, the physical SIM stops working.

## PIN and PUK
The default SIM PIN is 0000. After three wrong PIN attempts, the SIM is locked and the PUK code is needed.
The PUK code is shown in the NovaTel app (Account > SIM > PUK). After ten wrong PUK attempts, the SIM is permanently blocked and must be replaced.
