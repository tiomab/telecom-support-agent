# Roaming

## EU roaming (Roam Like at Home)
In all EU/EEA countries, calls, SMS and data are charged as at home, using the domestic plan allowance.
Fair-use data limit in the EU: Nova Start 6 GB, Nova Plus 15 GB, Nova Unlimited 25 GB per month. Above the limit, data costs 0.002 EUR per MB.

## Roaming outside the EU
Roaming outside the EU is charged per use unless a Travel Pass is active.
Pay-per-use rates: calls 1.50 EUR per minute, SMS 0.40 EUR, data 10 EUR per 100 MB.
Countries are grouped into zones:
- Zone 1: Turkey, Switzerland, United Kingdom, Serbia, Moldova
- Zone 2: USA, Canada, United Arab Emirates, Israel
- Zone 3: rest of the world

## Travel Pass
A Travel Pass is a daily package activated automatically on the first use in the destination zone.
- Zone 1 Travel Pass: 5 EUR per day — 1 GB data, 60 minutes, 60 SMS
- Zone 2 Travel Pass: 9 EUR per day — 1 GB data, 60 minutes, 60 SMS
- Zone 3: no Travel Pass, pay-per-use only
Travel Pass must be enabled once in the NovaTel app (Roaming > Travel Pass) before travelling.

## Enabling roaming
Roaming is enabled by default on all postpaid plans. It is disabled for accounts with an overdue balance.
If roaming does not work abroad, check that data roaming is turned on in the phone settings and try manual network selection.
