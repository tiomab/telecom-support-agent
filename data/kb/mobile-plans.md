# Mobile Plans

## Plan overview
NovaTel offers three mobile plans. All prices are monthly and include VAT.

| Plan | Price | Data | Calls & SMS (domestic) | 5G |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nova Start | 6 EUR | 10 GB | Unlimited | No |
| Nova Plus | 12 EUR | 50 GB | Unlimited | Yes |
| Nova Unlimited | 20 EUR | Unlimited (fair use 300 GB) | Unlimited | Yes |

## Changing your plan
Customers can upgrade their plan at any time; the upgrade takes effect immediately and the price difference is charged pro rata for the rest of the billing month.
Downgrades take effect from the first day of the next billing month.
Plan changes can be made in the NovaTel app (Account > My plan) or by contacting support.

## Running out of data
When the monthly data allowance is used up, speed is reduced to 128 kbps until the next billing month.
Customers can buy an extra data pack: 5 GB for 3 EUR or 20 GB for 8 EUR, valid until the end of the billing month.
Nova Unlimited customers who exceed the 300 GB fair-use limit are throttled to 10 Mbps.

## 5G availability
5G is available on Nova Plus and Nova Unlimited plans in Brasov, Bucharest, Cluj and Timisoara city centres.
A 5G-capable device and a SIM card issued after January 2023 are required. Older SIM cards can be replaced free of charge.
