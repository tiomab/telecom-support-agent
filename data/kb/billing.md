# Billing and Payments

## Billing cycle
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are due by the 15th.
Invoices are available in the NovaTel app (Account > Invoices) and are sent by email.

## Payment methods
Card payment in the app, direct debit, bank transfer, or cash at partner shops.
Direct debit customers get a 1 EUR monthly discount.

## Late payment
If an invoice is not paid by the due date, a reminder is sent and a late fee of 2 EUR is added.
If the balance is overdue for more than 15 days, outgoing calls, data and roaming are suspended; incoming calls and emergency calls remain available.
Services are restored automatically within 2 hours after the overdue balance is paid.

## Disputing a charge
Customers who believe a charge is wrong can dispute it within 60 days of the invoice date.
A billing ticket is opened and the disputed amount is put on hold (no late fee) while it is investigated. Investigation takes up to 10 working days.
