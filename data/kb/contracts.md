# Contracts and Cancellation

## Contract terms
Mobile and fibre plans are offered either without commitment (month to month) or with a 24-month commitment that includes a 20% discount.

## Cancelling a contract
Month-to-month contracts can be cancelled with 30 days' notice and no fee.
Cancelling a 24-month contract early costs the remaining discount received, at most 60 EUR.
Cancellation requests must be confirmed by the account holder and cannot be completed through the support chat; the agent should direct the customer to the app (Account > Contract > Cancel) or to a NovaTel shop.

## Number portability
Customers leaving NovaTel can keep their phone number. Porting takes 1 working day after the new operator submits the request.
